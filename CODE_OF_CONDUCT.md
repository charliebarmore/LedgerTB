# Code of Conduct

LedgerTB is committed to a respectful, professional, and welcoming community.
We expect participants to communicate constructively, assume good intent, give
actionable feedback, and respect differences in experience and perspective.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not
share client records, credentials, or other confidential financial information
in project spaces.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, project
events, and other spaces where someone is representing the LedgerTB community.

Report conduct concerns privately to **info@ledgerlabs.co**. Include relevant
links or context, but do not forward unrelated personal or client information.
Maintainers will review reports in good faith and may edit or remove content,
close discussions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when appropriate. Reports and reporter identities will be shared
only as needed to investigate and respond.
