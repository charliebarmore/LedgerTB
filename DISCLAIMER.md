## AI and assistant features

AI-generated suggestions and agent actions can be incomplete, inaccurate, or
inappropriate. LedgerTB's access levels and review surfaces reduce risk; they
do not replace human supervision. At the `post` access level, an assistant may
append balanced journal entries without prior approval. A person remains
responsible for reviewing that activity, making corrections through visible new
entries, and recording any professional signoff.

AI categorization and MCP assistant access are optional. When you enable them,
selected data may be sent by LedgerTB or by your MCP client to the provider you
configured. You are responsible for approving that provider and its terms,
retention, training, security, and data-handling practices for the information
involved.
